# References

This is a list of useful and classic textbooks, some of which may or may not be referenced in lectures or assignments. With the exception of Freeze and Cherry (1979), you are certainly not expected to read or acquire all of them!

Freeze and Cherry (1979) is a classic and is chosen as the required text for this course due to being offered for free online with an open source license (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0). If you prefer a paper copy, they are widely available used online for an affordable price. The online version is available in 8 languages and counting! Fetter and Kreamer (2021) is a more modern text that covers many topics beyond what can be covered in an introductory course but it is not required (i.e., you can wait until you have a job to buy it!).

## Required

Freeze, R.A. and Cherry, J.A., “Groundwater”, Prentice Hall, Inc., 1979

*The online edition and PDF are acceptable to use for this course and are available at fc79.gw-project.org/*

## Recommended

C.W. Fetter & D. Kreamer, “Applied Hydrogeology”, Prentice Hall, 5th ed., 2021.

Mitchell, J. K., Soga, K., & O’Sullivan, C. (2025). Fundamentals of soil behavior. (Fourth edition.). John Wiley & Sons, Inc.
