### New (Online) Resource

The Freeze and Cherry (1979) textbook does not provide a lot of guidance on evaluating total head and gradients. Fortunately this was remedied by one of the authors (Cherry) who co-wrote a fantastic online textbook: _Conceptual and Visual Understanding of Hydraulic Head and Groundwater Flow._ Today's lecture used examples that were inspired by chapters 2, 3 and 4 of this book. If you don't feel confident on the topic, go through the material and work through the example problems provided in the by Cohen and Cherry (2020). It can be viewed using [this link](https://doi.org/10.21083/978-1-7770541-6-8) and has also been added to the {ref}`References List <references>`.
