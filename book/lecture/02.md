## Darcy's Law

Darcy found (_empirically!_) that flow thropugh pipes is proportional to two things: the difference in head over a specific length and the soil properties of the porous medium:

$$
Q = K \frac{\Delta h}{\Delta L} A
$$

where:
- $Q$ = volumetric flow rate [L^3/T]
- $K$ = hydraulic conductivity [L/T]
- $\Delta h$ = change in head [L]
- $\Delta L$ = length of flow path [L]

We can also write this in terms of the specific discharge (or Darcy flux):

$$
v = \frac{Q}{A} = K \frac{\Delta h}{\Delta  L}
$$
